Freelance work: data cleaning, content systems and outreach design

Three client projects where the job was the same: turn a messy starting point into something a small business can trust and reuse. Each one is described by its method, not its data.

How to read this page. Clients are anonymized and shared with their agreement. No names, contacts, rates, accounts or client data appear here. These pages describe what I built and how it worked. They do not report business results, because I have not verified any.

Cleaning and verifying a large contact dataset

Client: a business-services company doing B2B prospecting · 09/2026 · my role: data builder and quality lead

The problem

The client had a large set of business contact records with inconsistent spelling, free-text job titles and no way to tell verified values from guesses.

What I did

  • Standardized messy fields. Merged spelling variants of cities and companies, and grouped job titles by seniority and function.
  • Matched each company to its own website. I checked the site against the company name before accepting it, and rejected look-alike matches from other countries.
  • Labelled provenance. Every derived value carries its source and a confidence label. Where I could not support a value, the cell stays blank instead of holding a guess.
  • Audited with random samples. Re-checking a sample against live sources exposed two classes of error: websites that belonged to the wrong company, and records pointing at another business. I fixed both and re-ran the audit.
  • Packaged the result as filterable CSV and Excel files with a plain-language overview report.

Workflow

TriggerThe client needs a prospect dataset they can act on.
InputsRaw records in inconsistent formats.
What happensNormalize, match companies to websites, verify, label provenance, audit with samples.
OutputsVerified datasets with source and confidence columns, plus an overview report.

Content strategy, audit and a multi-platform content system

Client: a solo consultant in professional services · 09/2026 to 10/2026 · my role: content strategist and producer

The problem

A small audience, an out-of-date brief built on assumptions nobody had checked, and a website whose claims contradicted each other.

What I did

  • Audited the channels and the website, checked the key assumptions against primary sources, and replaced the old brief with a corrected plan.
  • Recommended a leaner budget and a lead platform based on where the audience actually was.
  • Produced five platform content packs. Each has a timeline, creative, tracking links and a filming sheet.
  • Built a voice profile and a question bank from the client's own videos and audience questions.
  • Added a pre-publish review check that blocks a piece of content until its factual claims are supported, and re-ran it after every edit.

Workflow

InputsThe client's channels, website, video transcripts and primary sources.
What happensVerify, plan, produce, run the review check, hand over.
OutputsStrategy documents and ready-to-film content packs for five platforms.

One-variable cold-email pilot: design and sending setup

Client: a business-setup services startup · 10/2026 · my role: campaign designer and adviser

The problem

The client had no outbound channel, and draft copy that was too long and changed several things at once, so no result could be traced to a cause.

What I did

  • Designed a two-arm pilot that changes one thing only, with short follow-ups, so a difference between the arms has one explanation.
  • Specified the sending setup: authenticated domain records, reply detection, and automatic removal of anyone who replies, unsubscribes or bounces.
  • Scored the existing copy against published benchmarks and wrote shorter, fact-checked alternatives.
  • Added pre-send checks and stop rules: an anti-spam-law check, a slow ramp-up, and stop conditions on bounces and complaints.

Workflow

TriggerThe client approves the copy and the plan.
InputsA filtered contact list and approved copy.
What happensSlow daily sends on set weekdays, a reply pauses the sequence, weekly review against success rules set in advance.
OutputsReplies and booked calls, judged against those pre-set rules.
